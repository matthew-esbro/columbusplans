How it works

Verified, not vibes

Hours, prices and dates are checked against each venue's own site. Anything we could not confirm is flagged with a verify tag rather than guessed at.

Refreshed five days ahead

Each day's page is re-researched five days before that day arrives, so the calendar you see is the current one, not last month's.
